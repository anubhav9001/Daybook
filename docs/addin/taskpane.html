Tip: keep Daybook one click away

  • On the ribbon: choose Apps (on the Home tab, or under ⋯), right-click Daybook, and choose Pin. Suggest reply and Summarize then stay on the ribbon.
  • This panel: choose the pin at the top of the panel to keep it open as you move between emails. Daybook’s shortcuts (Ctrl+Shift+Alt+D and S) work while it’s pinned.

Daybook needs to be running on this PC

The Daybook add-in works with the Daybook app for Windows. Open this email in Outlook on a Windows PC where Daybook is installed.

  1. Install Daybook for Windows (free): Get Daybook.
  2. Open Daybook → Settings → Outlook add-in, turn on Allow the Outlook add-in, and choose Save settings.
  3. If Outlook or your browser asks to allow access to devices on your local network, choose Allow. Daybook runs on this PC, and that is how the add-in reaches it. Nothing goes to other devices.

Connect to Daybook

  1. Open Daybook on this PC → Settings → Outlook add-in.
  2. Turn on Allow the Outlook add-in and choose Pair Outlook.
  3. Type the 6-digit code shown there.
