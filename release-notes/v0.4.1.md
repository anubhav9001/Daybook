## Daybook 0.4.1

This release brings free, private AI that runs on your own computer, plus more cloud choices for open AI models.

### New: built-in private AI (from 0.4.0)
- Daybook now includes an open-source AI engine (llama.cpp). On the dashboard, choose **Set up private AI**: Daybook suggests a free Qwen3 model sized for your computer, downloads it once (2.5 GB or 5 GB), checks it for tampering, and switches reply suggestions to it automatically.
- No account, API key, or cost. Email is analyzed on your computer and never sent to an AI company. After the download it works offline.
- Interrupted downloads resume. You can remove the model any time in **Settings**.

### New in 0.4.1
- **Lighter on your PC:** the built-in AI runs at lower priority so the apps you're using come first.
- **Settings › Built-in private AI** now lets you choose when it frees memory after a suggestion (right away, 2, 10 or 30 minutes) and how much processor it uses (Light, Balanced or Full speed).
- **More cloud options for open models, with nothing to download:** Cerebras (free trial, very fast), SambaNova Cloud (free tier), Mistral (free Experiment plan) and Hugging Face (pay as you go). Each needs its own API key; see **Help › AI and privacy**.

### Fixes
- Groq requests that are too large now retry with a smaller request as intended.

### Good to know
- The model download needs access to huggingface.co. Some office networks block it; ask IT to allow huggingface.co and hf.co.
- Without a graphics card, a suggestion from the built-in AI can take about a minute.
- Mistral's free plan may use requests to train its models; use a paid plan for private email.
- The installer is still unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Install `Daybook-Setup-0.4.1.exe` below. If you already have Daybook installed, it will offer this update automatically.
