## Daybook 0.5.0

A smarter inbox, faster private AI, and a cleaner dark theme.

### Inbox
- **Sorted by your private AI.** With the built-in AI installed, Daybook reads the sender, subject, and preview of new messages on your computer and decides which need a reply. Each message shows the reason. Nothing is sent to an AI company.
- **Waiting on others.** A new tab lists conversations where you sent the last message and nobody has replied after a few days (2, 3, 5, or 7; choose in **Settings**). **Draft follow-up** writes a polite reminder to the same people and saves it as a draft.
- **Done and Snooze.** Clear a message from Needs a reply, or hide it until later today, tomorrow morning, or next week. This changes only Daybook's view, never your mailbox.
- **Search.** Type to filter what's on screen, or press Enter (or Ctrl+F) to search every connected account.

### Replies
- **Faster on-device replies.** The reply appears as it is written. The AI loads when you point at **Suggest reply**, and quoted email history is removed so there is less to read. If your computer is slow with the larger model, Daybook suggests the faster Qwen3 4B.
- **Tone, length, and language.** Choose formal, friendly, or brief; short to detailed; and the reply language, including Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, and Telugu. Then choose **Analyze again**.
- **Saved replies.** Keep reusable text in **Settings** and insert it into any draft.

### Calendar
- **Invitees' availability.** Suggested meeting times avoid your invitees' busy times when their calendars are shared with you, usually people in your organization. Daybook tells you whom it could not check.
- **Fix:** Outlook meeting times could appear shifted by your time-zone difference. They now show correctly.

### Always on hand
- **System tray.** Closing the window keeps Daybook running in the tray, so the morning brief and notifications keep working. Turn this off in **Settings**. Right-click the tray icon and choose **Quit Daybook** to close it completely.
- **Priority senders.** Get a notification when someone on your list emails. Add an address or a whole domain in **Settings**. Notifications never show message content.
- **Snoozed email** notifies you when it comes back.

### Look and feel
- **Clearer dark theme:** neutral grays instead of green-tinted backgrounds, with green kept for highlights.
- Message actions sit on their own row, so long subjects stay readable.

### Good to know
- **Google users:** to let Daybook check invitees' availability, disconnect your Google account and connect it again once. Everything else keeps working without reconnecting.
- If Daybook ever closes unexpectedly while the built-in AI is running, the next start cleans up the leftover AI process.
- The installer is still unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Install `Daybook-Setup-0.5.0.exe` below. If you already have Daybook installed, it will offer this update automatically.
