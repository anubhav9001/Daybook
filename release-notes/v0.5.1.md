## Daybook 0.5.1

Replies ready sooner, a calmer reply window, and a clearer calendar choice.

### Faster private replies
- **Replies prepared in the background.** The built-in private AI now drafts replies for the newest messages that need one while you work, at low priority. When a reply is ready, the message shows **Reply ready**, and opening it shows the draft straight away. Prepared replies stay in memory only and are never saved to disk. Turn this off in **Settings › Inbox and notifications**.
- **Less to write.** The built-in AI no longer reads your calendar or writes invitee notes for a reply; meeting times still come from your real calendars when you plan a meeting. New replies finish noticeably sooner on computers without a graphics card.
- Tip: for the quickest replies, choose **Qwen3 4B** and **Full speed** under **Settings › Built-in private AI**.

### Accessibility
- **Calmer reply window.** The reply now appears all at once when it is ready, with a quiet progress bar meanwhile. Screen readers hear one announcement when writing starts and one when the reply is ready, instead of constant updates. The progress bar stays still if Windows is set to reduce motion.
- Prefer to watch the reply being written? Turn on **Show the reply while it is being written** in **Settings › Inbox and notifications**.

### Calendar
- **Choose the calendar clearly.** **Plan a meeting** now lists every connected calendar in full (for example "Outlook calendar (Microsoft 365) · you@example.org"), instead of a narrow drop-down that cut the address off. It starts with the account the email came from, or the account you are viewing.

### Good to know
- The installer is still unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Install `Daybook-Setup-0.5.1.exe` below. If you already have Daybook installed, it will offer this update automatically.
